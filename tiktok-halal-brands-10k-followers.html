TIKTOK FOR HALAL BRANDS

❌ THE PROBLEM

TikTok is full of dancing, music, and haram content.

As a Muslim, you feel like you don’t belong. But TikTok has 1.5 billion users and Nigerians are blowing up daily. If you ignore it, you’re ignoring free traffic for your halal business.

✅ THE SOLUTION

Use TikTok the Halal way. No music, no dancing, no showing off. Just value + authenticity. TikTok algorithm rewards helpful content, not just trends.

Goal: 10k followers in 90 days + sell your halal products/services.

10 CONTENT IDEAS THAT GROW HALAL TIKTOK

1. "3 Duas for Rizq" Series

Text on screen + nature video. 15 seconds. Post daily after Fajr.

2. Islamic Book Recommendations

Hold book, flip pages, voiceover: "This changed my life". Link in bio.

3. Halal Business Tips

"3 mistakes I made selling on Jumia" - Screen record + talking hands only

4. Before/After: Canva Design

Show how you make Islamic Instagram post in 60 seconds

5. "Day in Life of Muslim Entrepreneur"

POV: Fajr, work, dhikr, family. No face needed. Hands and voice only.

6. Myth Busting

"No, you don’t need riba to start business. Here’s how..."

7. Product Reviews

"I bought this prayer mat from Jumia. Honest review."

8. Islamic History in 60s

"The story of Bilal RA in 60 seconds" - images + voiceover

9. Q&A: Halal Money Questions

"Can I do affiliate marketing?" Answer in 30 seconds.

10. Ramadan Prep Series

"30 days to Ramadan: Day 1" - builds massive anticipation

4 STEPS TO 10K FOLLOWERS

  • Step 1: Post 1 video daily for 90 days. Same time. Consistency wins.
  • Step 2: Hook in 3 seconds: "Muslims, stop doing this..."
  • Step 3: Use 3 hashtags: #MuslimTikTok #HalalBusiness #IslamicReminder
  • Step 4: Reply to every comment in first hour. Algorithm loves it.

HOW TO EARN FROM TIKTOK HALAL

  • 1. TikTok Shop: Sell hijabs, books, prayer mats directly
  • 2. Affiliate: Put Jumia/Selar link in bio
  • 3. Services: "I design Islamic posts - DM me"
  • 4. Digital Products: "Free template in bio" → Sell full pack

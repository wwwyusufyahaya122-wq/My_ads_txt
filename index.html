5 Ways To Earn Halal Income Online From Home

2. Selling Digital Products

Create and sell ebooks, Canva templates, Islamic planners, dua journals, and online courses. Sell on Gumroad, Selar, and Payhip. 100% profit, no inventory.

3. Halal Affiliate Marketing + E-commerce

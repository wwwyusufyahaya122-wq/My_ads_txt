1. Payoneer - Best for Upwork and Direct Clients

Upwork pays directly to Payoneer. You get a free US bank account number.

Steps:

1. Sign up at Payoneer.com using your Nigerian BVN
2. Connect it to Upwork/Fiverr
3. Withdraw to your Nigerian bank in Naira or USD domiciliary account
Fee: $3 per withdrawal. Takes 2-3 days.

2. Binance P2P - Best for Fiverr and PayPal

Fiverr pays to PayPal. Nigerians can’t withdraw PayPal. So we use USDT.

Steps:

1. Receive payment in PayPal
2. Sell PayPal balance to trusted vendor for USDT
3. Sell USDT on Binance P2P for Naira to your bank
Note: Only deal with verified P2P merchants. This is 100% halal if no interest is involved.

3. Wise - Best for Direct Bank Transfers

If your client wants to send direct USD. Wise gives you US, UK, EUR bank details.

Steps:

3. Give to client. They send USD.
4. Convert and withdraw to Nigerian bank

4. Domiciliary Bank Account

GTBank, Zenith, UBA all offer USD accounts. Withdraw directly from Payoneer to this account with zero conversion loss.

PROMOTE WITHOUT LYING

❌ THE PROBLEM

Most marketers lie to make sales.

Fake reviews, fake urgency, hiding product flaws. As a Muslim, this is haram. But if you don’t hype, will anyone buy? Many Muslims quit affiliate marketing because they think "honest = no money".

✅ THE SOLUTION

The Prophet ﷺ was the best salesman because he was the most truthful. Halal marketing = Barakah marketing. When you are honest, Allah puts barakah in your sales and customers keep coming back.

Truth sells better than lies in 2026. People are tired of scams. Be the trusted Muslim voice.

7 RULES FOR HALAL PROMOTION

1. Only Promote What You Use or Trust

Don’t recommend a prayer mat you’ve never touched. Buy it, test it, then talk about it. Your experience = trust.

2. Mention Both Pros AND Cons

"This Quran app is great but the ads can be annoying." People trust you more when you’re balanced.

3. No Fake Scarcity or Urgency

Don’t say "Only 2 left!" when it’s not true. Instead say "Ramadan is coming, prepare early."

4. Disclose Your Affiliate Link

Say: "This is my affiliate link. If you buy, I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you." Transparency = Sunnah.

5. Never Exaggerate Results

Don’t say "This course will make you $10k in 7 days". Say "This course taught me X. It took me 30 days to earn my first $100."

6. Reject Haram Products Immediately

Even if commission is $500. Gambling, loans with interest, music apps, dating. Your rizq is with Allah.

7. Give Value First, Sell Second

Post 3 free tips, then 1 product recommendation. Example: "3 Duas for Rizq" post, then "I use this Islamic planner to track them"

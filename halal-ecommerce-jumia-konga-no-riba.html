4 STEPS TO START SELLING WITHOUT RIBA

Step 1: Register as Jumia/Konga Seller - Free

Go to seller.jumia.com.ng or sell.konga.com. Use CAC or just BVN. Takes 48 hours.

Step 2: List Only Halal Products

In product description write: "100% Halal, No Riba". Take your own pictures. Use keywords: Islamic, Muslim, Halal, Prayer

Step 3: Use Jumia Fulfilled or Konga Express

Send your stock to their warehouse. They handle delivery. You get paid to your bank in 14 days.

Step 4: Avoid "Pay Later" and Interest Offers

Jumia will offer you loans to boost ads. Decline. Use profit to reinvest instead.

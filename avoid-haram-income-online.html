How to Avoid Haram Income Online

Protect your Rizq. Avoid these 7 Haram online income sources:

  1. Riba: Interest from banks, crypto lending, loans
  2. Gambling: Betting, Forex with leverage, Crypto trading
  3. Haram Products: Alcohol, Pork, Adult content
  4. Deception: Scams, Fake reviews, Misleading ads
  5. Music Industry: Promoting music and concerts
  6. Insurance with Riba: Conventional insurance
  7. Multi-Level Marketing with Haram: Pyramid schemes

Always ask: "Is Allah pleased with this income?" Barakah comes from Halal only.

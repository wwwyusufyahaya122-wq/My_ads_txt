HOW TO AVOID HARAM INCOME ONLINE

❌THE PROBLEM
Many Muslims earn online without checking if it's Halal. Riba, gambling, scams and haram products destroy barakah from your Rizq.
✅THE SOLUTION
Learn the 7 major Haram income sources online and replace them with 100% Halal alternatives. Allah increases wealth from Halal.

7 HARAM INCOME SOURCES TO AVOID

1. Riba

Interest from banks, crypto lending, loans, and credit cards.

2. Gambling

Betting, sports betting, Forex with leverage, Crypto trading/gambling.

3. Haram Products

Alcohol, Pork, Adult content, Cigarettes, Music promotion.

4. Deception

Scams, Fake reviews, Misleading ads, Dropshipping fake products.

5. Music Industry

Promoting music, concerts, streaming haram content for money.

6. Insurance with Riba

Conventional insurance with interest elements.

7. Pyramid Schemes

MLM with Haram products or impossible promises.

Always ask: "Is Allah pleased with this income?" Barakah comes from Halal only.

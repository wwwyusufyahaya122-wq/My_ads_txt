5 STEP FORMULA TO RANK ON GOOGLE 2026

Step 1: Find What Muslims Are Searching

Use free tools: Google, TikTok, Pinterest. Search "Islamic + problem"
Examples: "halal side hustles", "islamic budget template", "how to avoid riba"

Step 2: Use This Blog Structure

H1: Big Problem - ALL CAPS
Problem Box: ❌ The struggle
Solution Box: ✅ The Islamic answer
H2: Steps, Lists, Tips
CTA: Back to home button
Author + Footer: Build trust

Step 3: Add Your Keyword 5 Times

Title, First paragraph, 1 H2, Image alt, Conclusion.
Example keyword: "halal affiliate marketing nigeria"

Step 4: Make It 1200+ Words

Google loves long, helpful posts. Add examples, stories, Quran/Ahadith. No fluff.

Step 5: Internal Links + Adsense

Link to your other posts. Add Adsense in the middle. More time on page = higher rank.

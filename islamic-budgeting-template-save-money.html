❌ THE PROBLEM

Money finishes before month ends.

Salary comes in, 2 weeks later it’s gone. No savings. No Sadaqah. No money for Hajj or Umrah. Most budgeting advice tells you to take loans with interest. That’s haram.

✅ THE SOLUTION

Use the Islamic Budgeting 5 Jars Method. Based on Quran: "Eat and drink, but waste not" [7:31]

Result: Save money, pay Zakat, give Sadaqah, and still enjoy life. 100% Halal, no riba.

THE 5 JARS ISLAMIC BUDGET RULE

Jar 1: Needs 50%

Food, rent, bills, transport, health. Essentials only.

Jar 2: Savings 20%

Emergency fund, Hajj, Umrah, marriage, business capital. Save before you spend.

Jar 3: Sadaqah & Zakat 10%

2.5% Zakat yearly. 7.5% monthly Sadaqah. "Charity does not decrease wealth" - Hadith

Jar 4: Learning & Iman 10%

Islamic books, courses, donations to masjid, supporting dawah.

Jar 5: Wants 10%

Halal enjoyment. Clothes, eating out, gifts. Guilt-free because other jars are full.

3 RULES TO MAKE IT WORK

  • Rule 1: Pay yourself first. Move money to jars on payday before spending
  • Rule 2: Use separate bank accounts or envelopes for each jar
  • Rule 3: Review every Friday. Make dua for barakah in rizq

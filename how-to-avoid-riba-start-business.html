START BUSINESS WITHOUT RIBA

❌ THE PROBLEM

Every "business advice" says: Go to bank and collect loan.

But as a Muslim, you know riba destroys barakah. Allah declares war on those who deal with riba [2:279]. You want to start business but fear you need interest to grow. Is it possible in 2026?

✅ THE SOLUTION

Yes. You can start and grow a business 100% Halal, Zero Riba. The Sahaba built empires without banks. You can too.

Principle: Start small, reinvest profit, use partnerships. Barakah > Big Loans.

7 HALAL WAYS TO FUND YOUR BUSINESS 2026

1. Bootstrapping - Start Small

How: Use salary/savings. Sell from home. Start online.
Example: ₦20k to buy 10 hijabs → Sell → Reinvest profit

2. Qard Hasan - Interest Free Loan

How: Borrow from family/friend with written agreement. Pay back same amount.
Dua: "May Allah bless the one who is lenient when selling, buying, and asking for debt"

3. Mudarabah - Profit Sharing

How: Investor gives money. You do work. Split profit 50/50. If loss, investor bears it.
Perfect for: E-commerce, food business

4. Musharakah - Partnership

How: You + Partner both put money + work. Share profit and loss.
Example: You do marketing, partner funds inventory

5. Murabaha - Cost + Profit Sale

How: Islamic bank buys item, sells to you at higher price. You pay in installments. No interest.
For: Equipment, car, stock

6. Crowdfunding - Community

How: 100 people give ₦5,000 each = ₦500k. Give them product discount in return.
Where: WhatsApp, Instagram, Selar

7. Pre-Sell - Customer Funding

How: "I’m making 50 prayer mats. Pre-order now ₦10k". Use money to produce.
100% Halal: Prophet ﷺ did Salam contracts

3 STEPS TO START THIS WEEK

  • Step 1: List skills you have. What problem can you solve?
  • Step 2: Start with ₦0-₦50k. Sell service before product.
  • Step 3: Reinvest 80% of profit for first 6 months. No salary yet.
